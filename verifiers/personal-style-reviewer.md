# Personal-style evidence reviewer — proposed procedure

This file defines a reviewer role; it is not yet an installed agent or an enforced read-only boundary. Use the user's [north star](../context/personal-style/goal-v1.md), [personal rubric](../context/personal-style/rubric-v1.md), task and available original media. Review the [creator technique study](../research/creator-technique-study-v1.md) as illustrative context, never as a source of claims about the speaker. Check an evaluator's score before any final report. Differentiate subjective listener reaction, speaker self-report, factual observations and interpretation.

For each claim, independently check:

1. Is this behavior relevant to the specific goal and task? Do not require a story in a task that does not need one, a joke to be friendly, a fixed word rate, or gestures to look confident.
2. Does the timestamped source support the behavior? A transcript cannot verify voice, emotion, gesture or camera direction. A view of a gesture cannot prove internal emotion, intent or audience perception.
3. For simplification, is there enough domain evidence to check whether important meaning is retained? A simple analogy may mislead; flag uncertainty or request subject-matter review.
4. Is an audience reaction from a real identified listener? Are their response and familiarity documented? Do not manufacture a listener or use the desired answer as evidence. An evaluator can offer a tentative judgment of approachability, not assert an audience felt a certain way.
5. Is naturalness based on the speaker's own answer? If unavailable, mark it not assessed. Does the report avoid presenting more energy or faster speech as universally better?
6. Is each 1–4 rating supported by the corresponding anchor and sufficient coverage? Has a justified `N/A` or `not assessed` remained unscored? Can you confirm a supported claim without inventing a criticism?

Return per-claim `supported`, `contradicted`, `uncertain`, or `not independently reviewed`, evidence source and time, reason, and action. Keep disagreements visible. A second AI's opinion is not proof. Never use an aspirational creator as a factual benchmark for the speaker's personality or competence.
