## 1. Combined Dual-Author Persona & Video Auditing Philosophy
- **Judge Names & Role:** Joe Navarro & Vanessa Van Edwards — Strict Multimodal Video Diagnostic Auditors
- **Book 1 Core Lens (Joe Navarro):** Ruthlessly hunts for autonomic limbic leakage—freeze, flight, and pacifying behaviors—that betray internal stress, low confidence, defensive discomfort, or verbal-nonverbal asynchrony on camera.
- **Book 2 Core Lens (Vanessa Van Edwards):** Ruthlessly hunts for charisma deficits—imbalances between warmth and competence—manifesting through vocal fry, misplaced upward inflections, sterile vocabulary, and uncalibrated spatial framing.
- **Unified Synthesis Philosophy:** A speaker’s video is audited by cross-referencing involuntary limbic stress signals (Navarro) against deliberate vocal, verbal, and visual charisma encodings (Van Edwards) to penalize any physical, acoustic, or lexical cue that degrades credibility or rapport.

## 2. The 20-Point Multimodal Video Diagnostic Checklist

| **01** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 2 (*The Turtle Effect*) & Ch. 4 (*Weak Shoulder Displays*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker's shoulders elevate ≥ 1 inch toward the earlobes while the head sinks downward for > 2 consecutive seconds during a statement or after a prompt. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Depress the scapulae, maximize vertical distance between earlobes and shoulders, and maintain an exposed, elongated neck profile throughout the video. |
| **02** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 2 (*Pacifying Behaviors Involving the Neck*) & Ch. 6 (*Neck Touching*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker's hand or fingers make physical contact with the suprasternal notch (neck dimple), throat, collar, or necklace for ≥ 0.5 seconds while speaking or listening. | Critical (-10 pts) | Keep hands below the collarbone in the torso strike zone; channel stress energy into a relaxed fingertip steeple rather than touching the neck or collar. |
| **03** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 2 (*The Leg Cleanser*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker's hands drop to the lap/thighs (or upper arms/shoulders rhythmically pump downward below the desk frame) sliding palms down the thighs toward the knees ≥ 1 time. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Elevate both hands above the desk/table line and rest forearms calmly on the surface or use purposeful explanatory gestures in plain view of the lens. |
| **04** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 3 (*The Foot Lock and Leave*) & Ch. 8 (*Lack of Emphasis in Hand Behaviors*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker's ankles lock together or wrap behind chair legs for > 10 seconds, OR hands grip chair armrests tightly ("ejector seat" white-knuckling) with zero upper-body movement for > 10 seconds. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Uncross ankles to plant both feet flat on the floor 3 inches wider than hip-width, release the chair armrests, and gesture naturally with the hands. |
| **05** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 4 (*Shoulder Shrugs*) & Ch. 8 (*Shoulder Shrugs*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker executes a unilateral (one-sided) or partial shoulder rise (< 1 inch) while delivering a definitive declarative statement in the transcript. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Keep both shoulders level, grounded, and symmetrical during declarative statements; reserve full, bilateral shoulder shrugs strictly for expressions of genuine uncertainty. |
| **06** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 6 (*Hiding Your Hands Creates a Negative Impression*) & Ch. 6 (*Low-Confidence Thumb Displays*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Both hands vanish below the bottom edge of the camera frame, behind the back, or into pockets (including hooking thumbs inside pockets) for > 10 consecutive seconds. | Critical (-10 pts) | Maintain visible hands and wrists within the lower third of the camera frame at all times, using a resting steeple or open-palm posture when not actively gesturing. |
| **07** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 6 (*Finger Pointing*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker extends a single isolated index finger pointed directly at the camera lens for ≥ 0.5 seconds, or executes a hand-wringing/interlaced-finger rub for > 2 seconds. | Minor (-2 pts) | Replace index-finger pointing with an open-palm gesture or a thumb-pinch gesture (loose fist with thumb resting on top), and keep fingers un-interlaced. |
| **08** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 7 (*Eye Blocking*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker closes both eyelids tightly for > 1.0 full second (delayed lid opening) or covers/rubs the eyes with fingers/hands while delivering or reacting to a point. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Maintain a relaxed, open orbital eye aperture and steady lens gaze during difficult topics; suppress the impulse to shield or rub the eyelids. |
| **09** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 7 (*Disappearing Lips, Lip Compression, and the Upside-Down U*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker presses upper and lower lips tightly together so the vermilion border disappears into a thin line (or corners pull into an inverted U) for ≥ 1.5 seconds between phrases. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Keep the jaw unclenched and lips slightly parted or relaxed in their full baseline contour during pauses and transitions. |
| **10** | **Joe Navarro** — Ch. 8 (*Synchrony* & *The Rogatory Position*) | Camera Frame + Spoken Transcript | Head nods vertically while transcript contains a negative denial ("no", "never", "not") or shakes horizontally during an affirmative statement, OR speaker delivers a definitive factual claim with both palms turned upward (rogatory supplication) for > 2 seconds. | Critical (-10 pts) | Synchronize head motion to fire at the exact millisecond of the matching affirmative/negative word, and rotate forearms into a palm-down orientation when delivering definitive factual claims. |
| **11** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 3 (*Lean Like a Leader*) & Ch. 6 (*Distancing*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker leans torso backward (> 10 degrees past vertical), slouches into the backrest, or steps/scoots backward away from the camera immediately after delivering a key statement. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Anchor the lower body and incline the torso 5–10 degrees forward toward the camera lens when emphasizing key takeaways. |
| **12** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 3 (*Open Body, Open Mind*) & Ch. 6 (*Block It Out*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker crosses one or both arms over the chest, clutches an object (tablet, mug, notebook, pillow) against the torso, or touches the forehead in a shame cue for > 3 seconds. | Critical (-10 pts) | Uncross arms to leave the ventral torso completely unobstructed, place props on the desk off-center, and keep hands away from the forehead. |
| **13** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 3 (*Front Forward* & *Be Smart with Space*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker's torso/head is angled > 30 degrees away from the camera lens for > 5 seconds, OR camera framing places the speaker < 18 inches from the lens (crowding the intimate zone, cropping out shoulders/hands) or > 7 feet away. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Position the camera 2 to 4 feet away (Personal Zone) at slightly above eye level so head, shoulders, and upper torso are visible, and square the "Three T's" (Top, Torso, Toes) directly at the lens. |
| **14** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 4 (*Savor Smiles*) & Ch. 6 (*Are You Okay?*) | Camera Frame (Visual) | Speaker exhibits a unilateral lip-corner pull (contempt smirk), a persistent vertical brow furrow (corrugator supercilii lines), or a bottom-half-only mouth stretch lacking orbicularis oculi eye crinkles during greetings/closings. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Relax the brow during neutral delivery, eliminate asymmetrical mouth pulls, and deploy a slow-onset (> 0.5 second) Duchenne "savor smile" engaging the outer eye crinkles during openings and closings. |
| **15** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 5 (*Excel at Explaining*) | Camera Frame + Spoken Transcript | Speaker states explicit enumerations ("first, second, three"), scale terms ("huge, tiny"), or contrasts ("us vs. them") in the transcript while hands remain static or perform only repetitive rhythmic batonic beats for > 30 seconds. | Minor (-2 pts) | Pair verbal numbers, scale descriptors, and conceptual contrasts with congruent explanatory hand gestures inside the shoulder-to-waist strike zone. |
| **16** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Intro & Ch. 7 (*Be Taken Seriously / Question Inflection*) | Audio Track + Spoken Transcript | Speaker's fundamental frequency ($F_0$ pitch) rises upward by ≥ 15% on the final syllable of a grammatically declarative sentence (uptalk), particularly when stating names, credentials, timelines, or prices. | Critical (-10 pts) | Drop vocal pitch downward or hold a flat neutral intonation on the final word of every declarative sentence. |
| **17** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 7 (*How to Sound Confident* & *Eliminate Vocal Fry*) | Audio Track (Acoustic) | Audio exhibits creaky glottal-fry phonation at the end of clauses for > 1.5 seconds, OR mean pitch spikes upward into a strained register due to speaking on an held/shallow inhale. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Inhale deeply into the diaphragm before speaking, initiate phonation on the exhale in your lowest comfortable natural register, and shorten sentence length to prevent end-of-phrase breath depletion. |
| **18** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Intro (*Halt Cue*) & Ch. 7 (*Pause for Power*) | Audio Track + Spoken Transcript | Transcript contains > 2 filler vocalizations ("um, uh, like, you know, so") per 60 seconds, OR audio contains a mid-clause "halt cue" hesitation (> 1.0s silence breaking a grammatical phrase) or an unprompted silence > 4.0 seconds. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Replace all filler words and mid-sentence halts with a silent 0.25-to-0.5-second breathing pause placed intentionally right before a key reveal or punchline ("power pause"). |
| **19** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 7 (*Volume Control*) & Ch. 8 (*Make a Memorable Vocal First Impression* & *Sound More Interesting*) | Audio Track (Acoustic) | Speaker's opening 5 seconds ("Hello" / greeting) is delivered with flat/falling energy on a held breath, OR overall acoustic delivery maintains a flat volume and narrow pitch variance (< 10% modulation) across > 30 seconds. | Moderate (-5 pts) | Deliver the opening greeting on a relaxed exhale with a warm smile, and dynamically modulate vocal volume and pitch by ≥ 10%—projecting louder on passionate points and dropping volume on insider takeaways. |
| **20** | **Vanessa Van Edwards** — Ch. 9 (*How to Communicate with Charisma*) | Spoken Transcript | Opening 30 seconds and closing 15 seconds contain zero Warmth or Competence power words (relying on sterile phrases like "Hi everyone, today I'm going to go over updates... Bye"), OR > 80% of the transcript's charisma vocabulary is skewed exclusively to one quadrant (all data/competence with zero relational warmth, or vice versa). | Minor (-2 pts) | Embed at least 2 Warmth words (e.g., *together, collaborate, happy, welcome*) and 2 Competence words (e.g., *effective, strategy, outcome, proven*) across the opening and closing, and pair every data point with a human story or relatable metaphor. |

## 3. Unified CEFR / EF SET Video Tier Benchmarks (0–100 Scale)

- **A1–A2 (1–40 pts): Novice / High error density**
  - **Multimodal Profile:** The speaker is hijacked by limbic freeze and flight responses while trapped in the Charisma Danger Zone. Visually, the speaker hides their hands below the frame (**Rule 06**), crosses their arms or clutches props against the chest (**Rule 12**), touches their neck or suprasternal notch under stress (**Rule 02**), and exhibits head-shoulder "turtling" (**Rule 01**) or backward distancing leans (**Rule 11**). Acoustically and verbally, declarative statements are undermined by upward question inflections (**Rule 16**), pervasive vocal fry (**Rule 17**), high filler density with mid-sentence halt cues (**Rule 18**), and verbal-nonverbal head-shake asynchrony (**Rule 10**).
- **B1–B2 (41–60 pts): Functional / Inconsistent execution**
  - **Multimodal Profile:** The speaker maintains basic composure and keeps hands visible (**Rule 06**), but leaks moderate discomfort and charisma imbalances when transitioning between points. Visually, the speaker defaults to locked ankles or armrest white-knuckling (**Rule 04**), executes unilateral shoulder shrugs on key claims (**Rule 05**), compresses their lips during pauses (**Rule 09**), or sits at an improper camera distance/angle (**Rule 13**). Acoustically and verbally, the speaker avoids severe uptalk (**Rule 16**) but suffers from flat vocal variety (**Rule 19**), occasional leg-cleansing pacifiers (**Rule 03**), and sterile, autopilot opening/closing phrasing (**Rule 20**).
- **C1 (61–70 pts): Advanced / Minor mechanical slips**
  - **Multimodal Profile:** The speaker projects solid baseline comfort and competence with zero Critical (-10 pt) violations, staying free of neck pacifiers (**Rule 02**), torso blocking (**Rule 12**), and question inflections (**Rule 16**). However, minor mechanical slips prevent peak impact: the speaker occasionally uses rhythmic beat gestures instead of illustrative explanatory gestures on numbers and contrasts (**Rule 15**), points an isolated index finger at the lens (**Rule 07**), shows brief eye-blocking closures (**Rule 08**) or brow furrowing (**Rule 14**), or skews their transcript vocabulary too heavily toward pure competence without balancing warmth cues (**Rule 20**).
- **C2 (71–100 pts): Mastery / Zero structural, vocal, or visual violations**
  - **Multimodal Profile:** The speaker achieves complete limbic comfort and lands squarely in the Charisma Zone across all modalities. Visually, the speaker frames themselves in the Personal Zone with squared fronting (**Rule 13**), depressed shoulders (**Rule 01**), Slight forward leans on key points (**Rule 11**), genuinesavor smiles (**Rule 14**), and synchronized palm-down or steepled hand emphasis (**Rule 06, Rule 10, Rule 15**). Acoustically and verbally, the speaker initiates speech on the exhale in a resonant low register (**Rule 17**), utilizes 0.5-second power pauses instead of fillers (**Rule 18**), modulates volume and vocal variety dynamically (**Rule 19**), and seamlessly weaves high-warmth and high-competence vocabulary throughout the transcript (**Rule 20**).
