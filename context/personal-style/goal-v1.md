# My communication north star — draft v1

**Your long-term goal, based on what you shared:** Explain complex ideas in a simple, accurate, engaging way, so that the listener feels included in a friendly conversation; use stories, examples, and expressive delivery in a way that feels natural to you.

This is your own direction, assembled from qualities you admire in different creators. It is an initial interpretation to refine together. It is separate from English proficiency and the broader communication rubric, and it does not require copying anyone's accent, personality, speed, appearance, gestures or scripted production style.

## Personal style scorecard

The detailed [personal style rubric](rubric-v1.md) proposes 1–4 anchors for: approachable connection; simplifying accurately; concrete explanation; narrative movement; expressive delivery; and natural fit for you. This supplements rather than replaces the general rubric. Some categories need audio, video, a listener, a story-appropriate prompt, or your own report; without those inputs, they are not assessed. Scores are provisional, custom and unvalidated.

## Proposed first milestone — four weeks after baseline

On **two of three fresh comparable 90–120 second explanations**, aim to:

1. Identify the intended audience and communicate one main idea with a simple explanation and a relevant concrete example.
2. Have a listener who has not seen the intended point describe it back in their own words. Track each response as matched, partly matched, not matched, or unable to judge; compare with the intended point written before recording.
3. Ask that listener whether the talk felt approachable and easy to follow, without prompting them to use a particular adjective. Record their response and role.
4. After speaking, note whether the delivery felt natural to you and choose one moment to rehearse.

This is a proposed practice target, not your baseline or a promise of improvement. Two recordings do not prove the skill generalizes. Storytelling is a second practice track when the task calls for a story. A listener is optional: if none is available, listener understanding and interpersonal impression remain not assessed rather than simulated by the AI.

## What success could look like later

A person unfamiliar with the topic understands your main point and can give their own example; they find the explanation approachable; you can tell a short story that helps the idea; and you feel comfortable with how you express it. We will use comparable recordings and listener responses to test progress rather than treating a single score as a measure of your personality.
