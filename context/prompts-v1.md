# Prompt bank v1.0

Use 60–120 seconds, unscripted, first take, same preparation time (suggest 60 seconds), same recording arrangement, and comparable audience/task conditions. Mark any deviations. The proposed conditions are practice conventions, not scientifically validated thresholds. Rotate; do not compare a rehearsal to a first take. Prompts are similar but not identical in difficulty; record topic familiarity 0–10.

- **P1:** Explain a project you are working on to a colleague unfamiliar with it. State what it does and why it matters.
- **P2:** Explain a process you know to a colleague unfamiliar with it. State what happens and why it matters.
- **P3:** Explain a tool you have used to a colleague unfamiliar with it. State what it does and why it matters.
- **P4:** Explain a problem you encountered to a colleague unfamiliar with it. State what happened and why it matters.
- **P5:** Explain a concept you recently learned to a colleague unfamiliar with it. State what it means and why it matters.
- **P6:** Explain a change you observed to a colleague unfamiliar with it. State what changed and why it matters.

Before recording, write your intended main point privately; do not show it to the listener until after their answer. For a personal target where relevant, prepare two separate supporting points and a short close. Preserve the original prompt and its version.
